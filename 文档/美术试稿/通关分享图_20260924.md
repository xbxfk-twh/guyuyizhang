Composition MUST reserve a large calm near-uniform very dark navy central text-safe area from 22% down to 80% of total height, horizontal safe margins at least 16% each side; atmospheric faint fog gradient there but no distinct objects, no bright highlights, no bright shafts crossing the text area.
